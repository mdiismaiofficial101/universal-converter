Privacy Policy

Your privacy, explained clearly. Universal Converter is built to work without collecting your personal data.

Effective 18 Nov 2026 Last updated 18 Nov 2026 App version 1.1.0
01

Who we are

Universal Converter is developed by Ismail at CybroTechnology. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to the developer and company behind the app. This policy explains how the app handles your information. You can contact us at cybrotechnology1@gmail.com.

02

Information we collect

We do not collect, request or store your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, files or any other personally identifiable information. The app does not require an account, sign-in or profile.

Exchange rates are provided for informational purposes only and are not financial advice.

03

Data stored on your device

Your preferences live only on your device: theme, favourite and selected currencies, target-rate alerts, the last conversion category, notification settings and the first-run consent state. This data is never uploaded to us.

Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes this information from your device.

04

Network requests and third-party services

To show live exchange rates, the app sends requests containing currency codes to the Frankfurter API (api.frankfurter.dev) and, as a backup, the Open Exchange Rates API (open.er-api.com). These requests do not include personal information or an account identifier.

Like most internet services, those providers may receive technical data such as your IP address and process it under their own privacy policies. We do not control those services.

05

Notifications and permissions

With your permission (Android 13 and later), the app can send local notifications about significant exchange-rate movements and your target rates. Notifications are generated on your device. You can disable them in the app or in system settings at any time.

Permissions used: Internet, network state and notifications. The app does not use location, camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, call logs or storage permissions.

Background rate syncing is handled by Android WorkManager, which may declare system-level permissions such as wake lock and run-after-reboot. These only keep the scheduled sync reliable and never access personal data.

06

Ads, analytics and tracking

The app contains no advertising SDK, no advertising tracking and no third-party analytics. We do not build advertising profiles, track you across apps or websites, or sell data.

07

Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your personal information. Because we do not collect it, there is no server-side profile to disclose. We may disclose information only if required by applicable law; the app itself holds no personal data to disclose.

08

Security and retention

Your settings stay in the app's private storage on your device, protected by Android's app sandbox. We retain no personal data on our servers. Locally stored data remains until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Note: if you have enabled Android's system backup, your device may include app preferences in a backup managed by Google or your device manufacturer. That backup is outside our control.

09

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the app does not collect personal data, no such data is stored by us.

10

Your choices and data deletion

You can view or change your preferences in Settings. To delete all locally stored data, use Settings → Apps → Universal Converter → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact cybrotechnology1@gmail.com. Because we hold no server-side personal data, there is usually nothing for us to delete.

11

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect new features or legal requirements. The “Last updated” date will change, and material updates will be highlighted in the app. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

隐私政策

清晰说明你的隐私。Universal Converter 在不收集个人数据的前提下为你服务。

我们收集的信息

我们不收集、不请求、不存储你的姓名、电子邮件、电话号码、精确位置、联系人、照片、文件或任何其他可识别个人身份的信息。本应用无需账户、登录或个人资料。

你的偏好仅保存在设备上：主题、收藏和选中的货币、目标汇率提醒、上次使用的换算类别、通知设置以及首次使用同意状态。这些数据绝不会上传给我们。

网络请求与第三方服务

为显示实时汇率，应用会向 Frankfurter API（api.frankfurter.dev）发送包含货币代码的请求，并以防万一使用 Open Exchange Rates API（open.er-api.com）作为备用。这些请求不包含个人信息或账户标识。

与大多数互联网服务一样，这些提供商可能会收到你的 IP 地址等技术数据，并按其自身的隐私政策处理。我们无法控制这些服务。

05

经你允许（Android 13 及以上），应用可发送关于显著汇率变动和目标汇率的本地通知。通知在你的设备上生成。你可以随时在应用或系统设置中关闭。

使用的权限：互联网、网络状态和通知。应用不使用位置、相机、麦克风、联系人、短信、通话记录或存储权限。

后台汇率同步由 Android WorkManager 处理，它可能会声明唤醒锁和开机后运行等系统级权限。这些权限仅用于确保定时同步可靠，绝不会访问个人数据。

06

应用不包含广告 SDK、广告跟踪或第三方分析。我们不建立广告画像，不跨应用或网站跟踪你，也不出售数据。

07

我们不会出售、出租、交易或共享你的个人信息。由于我们不收集这些信息，服务器上也没有可披露的个人资料。仅在法律要求时我们才可能披露信息；应用本身不持有任何个人数据。

08

安全与保留

你的设置保存在设备上由 Android 应用沙盒保护的私有存储中。我们的服务器不保留任何个人数据。本地数据会一直保留，直到你清除应用数据或卸载应用。

Nota: si has activado la copia de seguridad del sistema Android, tu dispositivo podría incluir las preferencias de la aplicación en una copia gestionada por Google o el fabricante. Esa copia está fuera de nuestro control.

09

Privacidad de los menores

La aplicación no está dirigida a menores de 13 años (o la edad mínima exigida en tu país). No recopilamos a sabiendas información personal de menores. Dado que la aplicación no recopila datos personales, no almacenamos dichos datos.

Tus opciones y eliminación de datos

Puedes consultar o cambiar tus preferencias en Ajustes. Para eliminar todos los datos locales, usa Ajustes → Aplicaciones → Universal Converter → Almacenamiento → Borrar datos, o desinstala la aplicación.

Para preguntas de privacidad o solicitudes de eliminación, contacta con cybrotechnology1@gmail.com. Como no conservamos datos personales en servidores, normalmente no hay nada que debamos eliminar.
